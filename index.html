      About Davy

      Davy grew up just outside Denver, Colorado, picking up nearly every instrument he could get his hands on. He came to California to study voice and composition, earned a BM in music, worked in the recording studio of a major music publishing company and recorded two albums in a studio of his own.

      These days it's all about the live show: reading the room, building every set on his own guitar arrangements, and playing the right song at the right moment.
